## How do I add images?

Any of these work:

- **Paste** an image you've copied (<kbd>⌘V</kbd>).
- **Drag** image files onto the board — also straight from another website.
- Tap **+ → Image** (or press <kbd>I</kbd>) to pick from Photos or Files. On a phone or iPad it's called **+ → Photos**, and you can pick several at once.

Several photos at once arrive as a neat group, shaped to fit your screen, and Canvas glides to show them if they landed out of view. While they're being added you'll see "Adding 6 photos…", then how many made it.

Large photos are resized so boards stay quick to open and share. New items find free space, so they don't land on top of each other.

**Collecting on the go:** with your devices connected through [My Canvas](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device), open the board on your phone, add photos as you find them, and arrange them later on your iPad or computer.

## Is there a template to start from?

Yes, two, both free:

- [Room makeover moodboard](../../templates/room-makeover-moodboard/): the room today, the mood and the plan.
- [Client moodboard](../../templates/client-moodboard/): the brief, the mood, palette and type, and next steps.

Each opens as a new board of your own, with its slides already marked, so you can present it straight away.

## How do I tidy up a moodboard?

Select the things you want to arrange (drag a box around them, or <kbd>⌘A</kbd> for everything) and tap **Tidy up** in the bar above the toolbar. Canvas arranges them into neat columns with even gaps: photos get the same width and keep their shape, and small things like colour swatches sit side by side. Undo with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> if you liked it better before.

When you add several images at once (paste, drop or pick them), they arrive tidied up already.

## How do I crop an image?

Double-click (or double-tap) the image, or select it and tap the **crop** button in the bar above the toolbar. Drag the white corners or edges to choose what to keep, or drag inside to move the frame, then tap **Done** (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>). **Cancel** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves it as it was.

Canvas keeps the whole original, so you can crop again later and get back what you cut off — **Reset** shows the full picture again.

## How do I add a link?

Tap **+ → Link** and type or paste a web address — just `apple.com` is enough, no `https://` needed. You can also paste or drop a web address straight onto the board — `apple.com` works there too. (File names like `notes.md` stay text; if something becomes a link you didn't want, undo with <kbd>⌘Z</kbd>.) Canvas makes a **link card** with the page's preview image, title and site. Double-click (or tap) the card to open the page. Videos and music become cards that play on the board (see below).

## Can I add videos and music?

Yes. Add the link the same way, with **+ → Link** or by pasting it onto the board. You don't need to say what it is: Canvas recognises these, and the card becomes that service's own player, right on the board:

- **Videos:** YouTube (including Shorts), Vimeo, Loom and TikTok.
- **Music and podcasts:** Spotify, Apple Music, Apple Podcasts, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, Bandcamp, Deezer and Mixcloud.

Each card looks exactly like the player you know from Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud, at its usual size. Short links from the apps' Share buttons work too. Nothing plays by itself.

- **On your board:** tap the card once to select it, then use the player as normal (play, skip, scroll the track list). A press and drag on a card that isn't selected moves it.
- **People with a View link** can press play straight away, and so can you while presenting.
- The player grows and shrinks as you zoom. Players far off screen rest until you come back (one you've been using keeps playing).
- In PDF and PNG exports, a player shows as its cover picture with the title.

Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer play whole songs only for people who are signed in to them in that browser; everyone else hears a 30-second preview. Podcasts, YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, TikTok, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Mixcloud play in full (on Bandcamp, as far as the artist allows).

## Can I add a place, like a venue or a shop?

Yes. Tap **+ → Place**, type its name (for example "Tate Modern London") and press **Search**, then **Add**. Or paste a link from **Google Maps** or **Apple Maps** (their Share button works) into **+ → Place**, **+ → Link**, or straight onto the board. Canvas makes a **place card** that is a live map, right on the board: Google's own map for Google Maps links (with the place's name, rating and **Open in Maps** for directions), and an OpenStreetMap map for Apple Maps links, because Apple doesn't offer a map for other websites.

- **On your board:** tap the card once to select it, then drag, zoom or use the map. A press and drag on a card that isn't selected moves it.
- **People with a View link** can use the map straight away, and so can you while presenting.
- In PDF and PNG exports, the card shows a calm map picture with the place's name.

## How do I add a font?

Tap **+ → Font** and pick from 30 free typefaces: sans serif, serif, display, mono and handwriting. Each name is shown in its own font, and you can search. Add your own sample text if you like (a brand line works well), then **Add**. The card shows a big "Aa", your sample and the font's name, and it comes along in PDF and PNG exports. It's made for the palette and type part of a moodboard or brand board.

The fonts are stored on Canvas itself, so nothing is fetched from Google or other font services.

## Can I leave a voice note?

Yes. Tap **+ → Voice**, then the microphone, and talk for up to a minute. Tap the square to stop: the note lands on the board as a small card with its length. Tap it to play, tap again (or <kbd>Esc</kbd>) to stop. People with a View link can play it too, so it's a quick way to walk a client through a board.

Your browser asks once whether Canvas may use the microphone. It's only used while you record.

## Why does a link card have no picture?

Some websites — for example Dribbble, Behance and Unsplash — don't let other services read their previews, so Canvas shows a plain card with the web address instead. The link still works. Tip: to show the picture itself, save or copy the image and add it as an image.

## How do I add colour swatches?

Tap **+ → Colour**, pick a colour or type its hex code (like `#FF9F0A`), and tap **Add**. The swatch shows its hex code underneath. Select a swatch to change its colour.

## Can Canvas pick colours out of a photo?

Yes. Select a photo and tap **Palette** in the style bar. Canvas finds its main colours and adds them as a row of swatches under the photo.
