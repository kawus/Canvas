## How do I add images?

Any of these work:

- **Paste** an image you've copied (<kbd>⌘V</kbd>).
- **Drag** image files onto the board — also straight from another website.
- Tap **+ → Image** (or press <kbd>I</kbd>) to pick from Photos or Files.

Large photos are resized so boards stay quick to open and share. New items find free space, so they don't land on top of each other.

## How do I add a link?

Tap **+ → Link** and type or paste a web address — just `apple.com` is enough, no `https://` needed. You can also paste or drop a web address straight onto the board (starting with `https://` or `www.`). Canvas makes a **link card** with the page's preview image, title and site. YouTube and Vimeo videos show their thumbnail. Double-click (or tap) the card to open the page.

## Why does a link card have no picture?

Some websites — for example Dribbble, Behance and Unsplash — don't let other services read their previews, so Canvas shows a plain card with the web address instead. The link still works. Tip: to show the picture itself, save or copy the image and add it as an image.

## How do I add colour swatches?

Tap **+ → Colour**, pick a colour or type its hex code (like `#FF9F0A`), and tap **Add**. The swatch shows its hex code underneath. Select a swatch to change its colour.

## Can Canvas pick colours out of a photo?

Yes. Select a photo and tap **Palette** in the style bar. Canvas finds its main colours and adds them as a row of swatches under the photo.
