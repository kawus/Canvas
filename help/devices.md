## Where are my boards saved?

On your device, and in the cloud. Each board has its own link, and your browser keeps a list of your boards. That list is also backed up, encrypted, so you can bring it to your other devices with your **My Canvas link**. There's no account and no password.

## How do I get my boards on another device?

Use your private **My Canvas link**. Open the **board menu → Connect your devices**. You'll see a QR code and the link. Then, on the other device, either:

- **Scan the QR code** with its camera, or
- **Paste the link** — on the new device, open Canvas, then **board menu → Connect your devices → New device? Paste the link…**, and tap **Paste my link**. On Apple devices with the same Apple ID, you can copy on one and paste on the other.

You can also tap **Send to my other device** to AirDrop or message the link to yourself.

All your boards then show up in **All boards** on the new device, and you can keep editing them. If you have Canvas Pro, it comes along too.

## What happens to boards that are already on the new device?

They're kept. Connecting doesn't replace anything — the boards on both devices are combined into one list.

## Do my devices stay in sync?

Yes. Once connected, boards you create, rename or delete on one device show up on the others. Changes to a board appear on your other devices within about 10 seconds, even if the board is already open there; your list of boards refreshes whenever you open the board menu or All boards.

If you edit the same board on two devices at the very same moment, the most recent save wins — for real-time editing side by side, use [Edit together](topic:sharing#can-we-draw-on-the-same-board-at-the-same-time).

## I use Canvas from my Home Screen — how do I connect it?

The Home Screen app on iPhone and iPad keeps its own storage, separate from Safari, and links and QR codes always open in Safari. So connect it by pasting your link:

1. On a device that already has your boards, open **board menu → Connect your devices** and tap **Copy**.
2. Open Canvas from your Home Screen.
3. Open **board menu → Connect your devices → New device? Paste the link…** and tap **Paste my link**.

If you scanned the QR code and ended up in Safari, Canvas shows a note there with a **Copy my link** button — copy it, then follow steps 2 and 3.

## Do I need to connect each browser?

Yes, once. Each browser keeps its own list, so Safari and Chrome on the same Mac count as two devices, and so do Safari and the Home Screen app on an iPhone. Connect each one with the same link, and they all share one list.

## What if I connect with a different link by mistake?

Nothing is lost. Pasting a link merges this device's boards into that link's list. If you ever end up with two links, open one link on a device that has the other: everything joins one list.

## Is my My Canvas link safe to share?

No — keep it private. Anyone with it can see your list of boards and edit them, and use your Pro. Treat it like a house key: save it in your notes or password manager, but don't post it or send it to others. To share a single board, use the **Share** button instead.

## What if I lose the link?

As long as one of your devices still has your boards, you can get the link again: **board menu → Connect your devices**. If you lose every device *and* the link, there's no way to recover your list, because there's no account. That's why it's worth saving the link somewhere safe.

## What's the difference between “Delete board” and “Remove from my boards”?

- **Delete board** (boards you own) deletes it for everyone you shared it with, and takes it off your list on all your devices. It can't be undone.
- **Remove from my boards** (boards someone else shared with you) only takes it off your list. The board itself stays.

If you open a removed board's link again, it comes back to your list.
