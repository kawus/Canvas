## How do I share a board?

Tap **Share** (top right), then **Copy link**, and send the link to anyone. They don't need an account. With the **View** link, they see the board as it changes and can make their own copy, but they can't change yours — so nobody overwrites anyone.

## Can we draw on the same board at the same time?

Yes, with **Edit together**. In **Share**, switch to **Edit together** and copy that link. Everyone who opens it can edit with you, live. You see each other's cursors with names, what others are selecting and drawing, and who's there (their avatars at the top).

Editing together is part of [Canvas Pro](topic:pro). Only the person who owns the board needs Pro — people you invite join for free.

## How do I change the name others see?

When editing together, open **Share**. Under the link it says **You appear as** with your name. Tap the name to change it.

## What if two people edit the same thing?

When you edit together live, changes are merged item by item, so two people can work on different parts of the board at once. Undo only undoes your own changes.

## How do I edit my own board on another device?

Connect your devices with your [My Canvas link](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device) (**board menu → Connect your devices**): all your boards come along, with editing rights, and stay in sync.

## Someone shared a board with me. Can I change it?

If you opened a **View** link, the board is view-only for you: the toolbar is replaced by a **View only** bar. Tap **Make a copy** to get your own copy that you can edit; the original isn't changed. To edit the original together, ask the owner for an **Edit together** link.

## How do I stop someone from editing?

Anyone with the Edit together link can edit while the board is shared that way. To keep people out for good, make a copy (**board menu → Duplicate board**), keep working on the copy, and delete the original. Share View links when people only need to look.
