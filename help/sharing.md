## How do I share a board?

Tap **Share** (top right), then **Copy link**, and send the link to anyone. They don't need an account. With the **View** link, they see the board as it changes and can like things and leave short notes, but they can't change it, so nobody overwrites anyone.

## How do people give feedback on my board?

Send them the **View** link. When they tap a photo, note, colour or anything else on your board, two choices appear: **Like** and **Note**. A like shows as a small heart with a count; a note shows as a little pin with their first letter. The first time someone leaves a note, they type their name. There's no account, and they can't change the board itself.

Feedback is free, on every board that's in the cloud.

## Where do I see feedback?

When people have liked something or left a note, a speech-bubble button appears next to **Share**, with the number of open notes and a blue dot for new ones. Tap it to see every note, and which things people liked most. Tap a note and Canvas glides to it on the board. When you've dealt with a note, tap **Resolve** (or the tick in the list) and its pin goes away. **Show resolved** brings resolved notes back, and you can reopen them.

Your boards list shows **2 new** (for example) next to boards with notes you haven't seen yet.

## Can I turn feedback off?

Yes. Open the feedback list and switch off **Let viewers leave feedback**. People with the link then only see the board, without hearts or notes. Switch it back on at any time and the open notes return.

Feedback never appears in exports, presentations or link previews.

## Can we draw on the same board at the same time?

Yes, with **Edit together**. In **Share**, switch to **Edit together** and copy that link. Everyone who opens it can edit with you, live. You see each other's cursors with names, what others are selecting and drawing, and who's there (their avatars at the top).

Editing together is part of [Canvas Pro](topic:pro). Only the person who owns the board needs Pro — people you invite join for free.

## How do I change the name others see?

When editing together, open **Share**. Under the link it says **You appear as** with your name. Tap the name to change it.

## What if two people edit the same thing?

When you edit together live, changes are merged item by item, so two people can work on different parts of the board at once. Undo only undoes your own changes.

## How do I edit my own board on another device?

Connect your devices with your [My Canvas link](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device) (**board menu → Connect your devices**): all your boards come along, with editing rights, and stay in sync.

## Someone shared a board with me. Can I change it?

If you opened a **View** link, the board is view-only for you: the toolbar is replaced by a small bar at the bottom. If the owner has feedback turned on, you can like things and leave notes (see below). To get your own copy that you can edit, open the **board menu** (next to the board's name) and choose **Make a copy**; the original isn't changed, and notes you leave on your copy don't reach the owner. To edit the original together, ask the owner for an **Edit together** link.

## How do I stop someone from editing?

Anyone with the Edit together link can edit while the board is shared that way. To keep people out for good, make a copy (**board menu → Duplicate board**), keep working on the copy, and delete the original. Share View links when people only need to look.
