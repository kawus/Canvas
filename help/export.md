## How do I export a board?

Tap the **Export** button (the download arrow, top right) and choose:

- **PDF** — sharp at any size, great for printing and sending. Text stays text.
- **PNG** — an image, for slides, chats and documents.
- **SVG** — a vector file for design tools like Figma or Illustrator.

## Can I export only part of a board?

Yes. Select the items you want first, then open **Export** and tick **Only selection**.

## The share link is very long. What can I do?

On some boards a link can get long, and some chat apps cut long links off. Use an [export](topic:export#how-do-i-export-a-board) instead, such as a PDF.
