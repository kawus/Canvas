## How do I export a board?

Tap the **Export** button (the download arrow, top right) and choose:

- **PDF** — sharp at any size, great for printing and sending. Text stays text.
- **PNG** — an image, for slides, chats and documents.
- **SVG** — a vector file for design tools like Figma or Illustrator.

## Can I paste a picture of my board into a chat or an email?

Yes, without downloading anything. Open **Export** and choose **Copy as picture**, or press <kbd>⌘⇧C</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+C</kbd> on Windows). Then paste it into Slack, Messages, an email or a slide. With things selected, you get a picture of just those; otherwise the whole board. It works on a View link too. Like PNG exports, the picture carries the small “Made with Canvas” line unless you have Pro.

## Can I paste my notes into another app?

Yes. Select sticky notes, text or links, copy them (<kbd>⌘C</kbd>), and paste into an email, Notes or a document: you get the words, one note per line, in reading order (top to bottom, left to right). Pasted back into Canvas, on this board or another one, they come back as the notes themselves.

## Can I export only part of a board?

Yes. Select the items you want first, then open **Export** and tick **Only selection**.

Slide frames (the dashed outlines from [presenting](topic:presenting#how-do-i-make-a-presentation)) never show in exports.

## The share link is very long. What can I do?

On some boards a link can get long, and some chat apps cut long links off. Use an [export](topic:export#how-do-i-export-a-board) instead, such as a PDF.
