## Why does a board say “View only”?

You opened it with a View link, so you can look but not change it. Usually that's because someone shared it with you. If it's your own board:

- Make sure the device is connected with your [My Canvas link](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device). Your boards then open with editing rights.

Tap **Make a copy** to get your own editable copy of any board.

## I can't find a board

- Open **board menu → All boards** and use the search box.
- If it was made on another device, connect this device with your [My Canvas link](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device).
- On iPhone and iPad, Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate lists — check the other one, or [connect the Home Screen app](topic:devices#i-use-canvas-from-my-home-screen-how-do-i-connect-it).
- If you still have the board's link, open it — the board comes back to your list.

## My boards disappeared from Safari

Safari can clear a website's storage after a while without visits. Your boards are still in the cloud: open your My Canvas link (or paste it under **Connect your devices**) and they come back. Canvas also asks your browser to keep its storage, which makes this less likely.

## The cloud icon shows a warning

- **Offline:** you're not connected. Your changes are safe on this device and upload when you're back online.
- **Couldn't sync:** a temporary problem. Your changes are on this device, and Canvas tries again.
- **Too large to sync:** the board is too big to upload (usually lots of large images). It's saved on this device only — try removing a few images, or split the board.

## I can't see the other person's cursor

- Make sure you both opened the **Edit together** link, not a View link.
- Editing together needs the board's owner to have [Canvas Pro](topic:pro).
- A board disconnects after it's been in a background tab for about a minute. Switching back reconnects it.

## Paste my link doesn't work

Your browser may not allow reading the clipboard. Paste the link into the box below the button instead (press and hold, then **Paste**), and tap **Connect**. A My Canvas link contains `#me=`.

## Something else isn't working

Reload the page — your work is saved. If the problem continues, try the latest version of Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
