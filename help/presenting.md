## How do I make a presentation?

1. Move and zoom the board until you see what your first slide should show.
2. Tap **Present** at the top right, then **Add slide**. Canvas frames what's on your screen.
3. Do the same for the next part of the board, and the next.
4. Tap **Play from slide 1**.

There's nothing to copy or rebuild: a slide is just a window onto your board, so whatever you draw inside it shows up when you present.

## How do I play it?

Tap **Present → Play**. The show fills the screen and starts by zooming in from the whole board to your first slide. Then:

- **Next slide:** <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd>, or click/tap anywhere. On a phone or iPad you can also swipe left.
- **Previous slide:** <kbd>←</kbd>, tap the left edge of the screen, or swipe right.
- **Overview:** tap the grid button (or press <kbd>O</kbd>) to see the whole board with every slide numbered. Tap a slide to jump there.
- **Stop:** <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the **×** button.

Going from one slide to the next, Canvas pulls back far enough to show where you are and where you're going, then glides in. That way your audience sees how each slide fits into the bigger board. After the last slide you land on the overview, which works well for questions.

Presentation clickers work too, because they send the same keys.

## Can I start from a slide in the middle?

Yes. Select the slide on the board (tap its number) and tap **Present** in the bar above the toolbar. Or open **Present**, tap the slide in the list, and tap **Play from slide …**.

## How do I change a slide?

A slide shows on the board as a thin dashed frame with its number in a small circle. These only show while you edit, never in the presentation itself.

- **Move it:** drag its number (or its edge).
- **Resize it:** select it and drag a corner. Slides always keep the widescreen shape (16:9), so they fill a TV or projector neatly.
- **Change the order:** open **Present** and drag a slide by the dots on the right.
- **Delete it:** select it and press <kbd>Delete</kbd> or tap the bin. Only the frame goes; what's on the board stays.

Moving a slide doesn't move what's inside it. If you want to move a part of the board together with its slide, select both (drag a box around them) and move them together. You can also group them with <kbd>⌘G</kbd>.

## Do slides show in exports or link previews?

No. Slide frames only appear while you edit. PDF, PNG and SVG exports, and the picture in link previews, show just your board.

## Can someone else present my board?

Yes. Anyone with the board's link, even a view link, can tap **Present → Play**. Only people who can edit can add, move or reorder slides. Slides sync to your other devices like everything else on the board.
