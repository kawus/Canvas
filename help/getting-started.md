## What is Canvas?

Canvas is a calm, minimal whiteboard. You can sketch, write, add shapes, sticky notes, images and links, and share what you make. It works in your browser on a computer, iPad and iPhone, with a mouse, a trackpad, your fingers or Apple Pencil.

There's no sign-up. Open [Canvas](app:) and start drawing.

## How do I start a new board?

Every time you open Canvas you're on a board, with the Select tool picked so a stray tap never leaves a mark — pick the pen or another tool to start. To start another, open the **board menu** (the Canvas logo, top left) and choose **New board**. Give it a name by clicking **Untitled** next to the logo and typing.

## Do I need to save?

No. Everything saves automatically as you work, first on your device (so it also works offline) and then in the cloud. The little cloud icon next to the board's name shows where things stand:

- **Cloud** — saved. The board's link works on any device.
- **Spinning circle** — saving or loading.
- **Cloud with a line through it** — you're offline. Your work is safe on this device and uploads when you're back online.

## How do I find my other boards?

Open the **board menu**. Your most recent boards are listed at the top. **All boards** shows every board you have with a small preview, including ones you made on your other devices, and a search box once you have a few.

## How do I move around the board?

- **Scroll** with a mouse wheel or two fingers on a trackpad to move around. Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>) and scroll, or pinch, to zoom.
- **Drag with the Hand tool**, or hold <kbd>Space</kbd> and drag.
- On a **touchscreen**, drag with two fingers and pinch to zoom.
- The buttons in the bottom-left corner zoom in and out; the last one fits everything on screen (<kbd>⇧1</kbd>).

## How do I undo something?

Use the undo and redo arrows in the bottom-left corner, or <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> and <kbd>⇧⌘Z</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Z</kbd> on Windows). When you're editing together with someone, undo only undoes your own changes.

## Can I use dark mode?

Yes. Canvas follows your device's light or dark setting. To choose one yourself, open the **board menu** and pick **Auto**, **Light** or **Dark** at the bottom.

## Where do I find help inside Canvas?

Tap the **?** button in the bottom-right corner. It has this help centre, the welcome tour, and a list of shortcuts that fits your device: keyboard shortcuts on a computer, touch and Apple Pencil gestures on an iPad (with a Keyboard tab for when one is attached), and touch gestures on an iPhone.

## Can I see the welcome tour again?

Yes — tap **? → Welcome tour**, or [show the welcome tour](app:?tour).
