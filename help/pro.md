## What do I get with Canvas Pro?

- **Edit together, live** — invite people to draw on your boards with you in real time, with cursors and names.
- **Guests join free** — only you need Pro, not the people you invite.
- **On all your devices** — Pro comes along with your [My Canvas link](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device).

Everything else — unlimited boards, View links, exports, images and moodboards — is free.

## How much does Pro cost?

<p data-pro-price>You'll find the current prices in Canvas: <strong>Share → Edit together</strong>, or <strong>board menu → Upgrade to Pro</strong>.</p>

## How do I upgrade?

Open **Share → Edit together → Upgrade to Pro**, or **board menu → Upgrade to Pro**. Choose monthly or yearly and continue.

## How do I use Pro on my other devices?

Connect them with your My Canvas link (**board menu → Connect your devices**). Pro turns on there automatically.

## How do I change or cancel my subscription?

Open the **board menu → Manage subscription**. This opens a secure billing page where you can switch plans, update your card or cancel. If you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Your boards stay yours either way — only live editing together stops.
