## What do I get with Canvas Pro?

Canvas Pro comes as a **Founder licence**: you pay once and keep it for good.

- **Edit together, live:** invite people to draw on your boards with you in real time, with cursors and names. Guests join free; only you need Pro.
- **No "Made with Canvas" mark:** your shared boards and your PNG, PDF and SVG exports go out clean.
- **Every Pro feature that comes later,** at no extra cost.
- **On all your devices:** Pro comes along with your [My Canvas link](topic:devices#how-do-i-get-my-boards-on-another-device).

Everything else is free: unlimited boards, view links, presenting, exports, images and moodboards.

## How much does it cost?

<p data-pro-price>You'll find the current price in Canvas: <strong>board menu → Canvas Pro</strong>.</p>

## Is it a subscription?

No. It's one payment, with nothing to renew and nothing to cancel. Stripe handles the payment and emails you a receipt.

## How do I become a founder?

Open the **board menu → Canvas Pro**, or tap **Remove it with Pro** in the Export menu, then **Become a founder**. You pay on Stripe's secure checkout page and come straight back to your board with Pro switched on.

## How do I use Pro on my other devices?

Connect them with your My Canvas link (**board menu → Connect your devices**). Pro turns on there automatically.

## What if I lose my devices?

Your Pro travels with your My Canvas link, so [back the link up](topic:devices#can-i-back-up-my-link-by-email) and Pro comes back with your boards. If you've lost the link too, reply to your Stripe receipt and we'll sort it out.

## Where are my receipts?

**Board menu → Billing & receipts** opens Stripe's secure billing page with your receipts and invoices.

## Why the "Made with Canvas" mark?

Canvas has no ads and no accounts. A small mark on shared boards and exports is how new people discover it. Founders can go without it, and help keep Canvas independent and private by design.
