## What's in the toolbar?

The toolbar at the bottom groups similar tools so it stays small, even on a phone:

- **Move** — Select <kbd>V</kbd> and Hand <kbd>H</kbd>
- **Draw** — Pen <kbd>P</kbd>, Highlighter and Eraser <kbd>E</kbd>
- **Shapes** — Rectangle <kbd>R</kbd>, Ellipse <kbd>O</kbd>, Arrow <kbd>A</kbd> and Line <kbd>L</kbd>
- **Sticky note** <kbd>N</kbd> and **Text** <kbd>T</kbd>
- **+** — add an Image, a Link or a Colour swatch

A group shows the tool you used last. Tap it again, or press and hold it, to pick another tool from that group (the small corner mark shows which buttons have more inside).

## How do I draw?

Pick the **Pen** and draw. Lines are smoothed, even when you draw fast, and react to pressure with Apple Pencil. The **Highlighter** draws wide, see-through strokes. The **Eraser** removes whatever you drag it across.

## How do I add shapes, sticky notes and text?

- **Shapes:** pick Rectangle or Ellipse and drag on the board. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> for a perfect square or circle.
- **Sticky notes:** pick Sticky note and tap where you want one, then type. Text wraps and shrinks to fit the note.
- **Text:** pick Text and tap anywhere, or double-click an empty spot on the board.

## Can I write inside a shape?

Yes. Double-click (or double-tap) a rectangle or ellipse, or select it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, then type. The text stays centred in the shape when you resize it.

## How do arrows connect to things?

Start or end an arrow on a shape, sticky note, image, text or link card, and it attaches to it. Move the item later and the arrow follows. Arrows that start or end on empty space stay where you put them.

## How do I change colours and sizes?

Select something (or pick a tool) and a small style bar appears above the toolbar. It has buttons for:

- **Colour** — 8 colours (sticky notes have their own 8 paper colours)
- **Stroke** — thin, medium or thick lines
- **Fill** — outline, tint or solid for shapes
- **Text size** — 4 sizes

The **⋯** button holds Duplicate, Bring to front and Send to back.

## How do I select, move and resize?

With the Select tool, click an item, or drag a box around several. Drag to move them; drag a corner handle to resize (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to keep the proportions). The arrow keys nudge a selection, and <kbd>⌫</kbd> deletes it.

To copy, use <kbd>⌘C</kbd> / <kbd>⌘V</kbd>, <kbd>⌘D</kbd> to duplicate, or hold <kbd>⌥ Option</kbd> while dragging.

## How does auto alignment work?

While you move, resize or draw, items snap to the edges and centres of things nearby, and pink guide lines show you what lines up. Pink markers appear when the gaps between items are equal.

- Hold <kbd>⌘</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>) while dragging to place something freely.
- Turn **Snap to objects** or **Snap to grid** on or off in the **board menu**. The grid works in steps of 8: things snap to 8 px at 100% (16 or 24 px when you're zoomed out), so positions and sizes are always multiples of 8. The background dots show the grid — 24 px apart at 100%, closer together as you zoom in. With the grid on, the arrow keys nudge by 8 (24 with <kbd>Shift</kbd>).
- With two or more items selected, the **Align** button lines them up (left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom) or spaces three or more evenly.
