## Does Canvas work with Apple Pencil?

Yes. Ink is pressure-sensitive and smooth, and the Pencil always draws — even when the Select tool is picked, so you never need to switch tools to sketch. A quick tap with the Pencil selects something instead, and dragging something that's already selected moves it. Once Canvas notices your Pencil, your fingers select and move things, one finger moves the board around on empty space, and a palm resting on the screen is ignored, so you can lean on the iPad while you draw.

## Is there a Pencil menu?

Yes. **Hold the Pencil still on the board** for a moment: a thin ring draws itself around the tip, then a ring of six choices opens right there. Going clockwise from the top: pen, highlighter, eraser, select, undo and colour.

- **Flick** the Pencil toward a choice and lift. You don't need to land on it exactly; the direction is enough, and the middle names what you're about to pick.
- **Colours:** move onto the colour segment and the colours fan out beyond it. Go out to one and lift. Back in the middle, they fold away again.
- Or **lift** first, then **tap** a choice. Tap anywhere else to close the menu.

The choices always sit in the same places, so after a while it becomes a quick flick. If you start drawing before the ring finishes, you simply draw. Picking a colour switches to the pen (or recolours the highlighter, if that's what you're using).

Some drawing apps open their menu when you squeeze an Apple Pencil Pro. Safari doesn't share the squeeze with websites yet, so in Canvas you hold the Pencil still instead.

## Which gestures can I use on a touchscreen?

- **Pinch** with two fingers to zoom.
- **Drag with two fingers** to move around (or with one finger, once you've used Apple Pencil).
- **Double-tap** a shape to type a label in it.
- **Press and hold** a toolbar button to see the other tools in its group.

## Can I write text with Apple Pencil?

Yes. Pick the Text tool and tap the board. The keyboard opens, and Scribble lets you handwrite text that turns into type.

## How do I put Canvas on my Home Screen?

In Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**. Canvas then opens full screen, like an app.

> **Good to know:** the Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. The first time you use it, connect it to your other devices so your boards appear there too — see [Connect the Home Screen app](topic:devices#i-use-canvas-from-my-home-screen-how-do-i-connect-it).

## Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. The toolbar and style bar are compact enough for a phone screen: tools are grouped, and colours and sizes open in small menus. The board menu, Share and Export are at the top.
