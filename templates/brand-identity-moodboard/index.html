Questions

Can the team give feedback without an account?

Yes. Send a view link: everyone sees the board as it changes, likes the directions they love and leaves short notes and replies right on the board, with just their name.

Can I sketch logo ideas?

Yes. Draw with the pen, a mouse or Apple Pencil on iPad, and hold at the end of a stroke to straighten circles, rectangles and lines.

Is it free?

Yes. Canvas is free to use, with no account and no trial.

Can I export it?

Yes: a crisp PDF, PNG or SVG of the whole board or just a selection.

Where are my boards stored?

On your device, and in the cloud so the link works anywhere. There’s no account, and your list of boards is encrypted so the server can’t read it.
