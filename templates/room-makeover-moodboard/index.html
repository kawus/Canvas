Questions

Is the template free?

Yes. Canvas is free to use, with no account and no trial.

Can I share it with my partner or a builder?

Yes. Send a view link: they see the board as it changes and can make their own copy. To plan together live, invite them to edit with you.

Does it work on iPad and iPhone?

Yes, in the browser, including Apple Pencil on iPad. Add Canvas to your Home Screen to use it full screen, like an app.

Can I add my own photos?

Yes. Paste them, drag them in from a website, or pick them from Photos. You can crop them and pull colour palettes out of them.

Where are my boards stored?

On your device, and in the cloud so the link works anywhere. There’s no account, and your list of boards is encrypted so the server can’t read it.
