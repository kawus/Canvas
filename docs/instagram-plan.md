# Canvas on Instagram: Reels release plan

**Goal (from your kill rule):** interest. That means visits to canvas.kawus.com, people trying it, sign-ups for updates and shares. Followers are a bonus.
**Format:** 7 vertical Reels, 10–24 seconds each, recorded from the real app. Each shows one thing a viewer can understand in a second.
**Safe for Instagram's interface:** the app plays in the middle band of the frame. The header area at the top and the caption, name and audio area along the bottom stay empty board colour, so hooks, captions and every tap stay visible. Instagram's button column on the right only covers the app's small "?" button.
**Rhythm:** 3 posts a week for 2½ weeks, alternating the angles: presenting, moodboards, Apple Pencil, home projects, designers, privacy.
**Times:** Copenhagen time. Weekdays at 12:00 (lunch scroll) or 19:00 (evening scroll); the home-project Reel goes out on Saturday morning.

---

## Before the first post (one time, about 20 minutes)

1. **Professional account:** Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools → *Switch to professional account* → **Creator**. This unlocks scheduling and insights.
2. **Name:** `Canvas · calm whiteboard`
3. **Bio:**
   > A calm whiteboard for sketching, moodboards and presenting.
   > No sign-up. Private by design. Mac, iPad, iPhone.
   > ↓ Try it free
4. **Links in bio** (Edit profile → Links, up to 5):
   - `canvas.kawus.com` (Try Canvas)
   - `canvas.kawus.com/templates/room-makeover-moodboard/` (Room makeover template)
   - `canvas.kawus.com/templates/client-moodboard/` (Client moodboard template)
5. **Profile picture:** the Canvas app icon (`public/icon-512.png` in the project).
6. **Sound:** the videos are silent on purpose. When you upload each Reel, tap **Add audio** and pick a calm, trending instrumental (lo-fi or soft piano) at low volume. Reels with music reach more people.

## How to schedule them (all 7 in one sitting)

In the Instagram app (professional accounts):
1. **+** → **Reel** → pick the video.
2. **Next**: skip editing, then add audio if you like (see above).
3. **Next**: paste the caption and hashtags.
4. **Edit cover** → *Add from camera roll* → choose the matching `…-cover.jpg`.
5. Add the alt text under **Advanced settings → Accessibility**.
6. **More options → Schedule this reel**, then set the date and time from the calendar → **Schedule**.

Or on a computer with **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) → *Create Reel* → upload, caption and cover → *Schedule*.

The first comment and the Stories can't be scheduled. Do them on the day; it takes 2 minutes each.

## What to watch (each Sunday, 10 minutes)

| Signal | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plays and **average watch time** per Reel | Instagram → Insights | Which hooks hold attention (aim for more than 70% of the Reel watched) |
| **Shares and saves** | Insights | The strongest sign of interest on Instagram |
| **Link taps** in bio and stories | Insights → Profile activity | Instagram turning into visits |
| Visits to `/intro/`, the two template pages, and `/made-with-canvas/` | Vercel → Analytics | Whether home projects or designers is your stronger audience |
| People on the updates list | canvas.kawus.com/#stats | Interest you can reach again |

**After Reel 7:** whichever of Reels 5 and 6 did better is your audience. Make the next round about that audience: more templates and more "before → after" Reels.

**Optional:** the same files work as TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Post them there with the same captions for extra reach at no extra effort.
